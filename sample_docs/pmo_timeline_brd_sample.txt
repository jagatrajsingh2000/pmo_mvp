Project Name: Customer Onboarding Portal

Overview:
The Customer Onboarding Portal will allow enterprise customers to submit onboarding details, upload compliance documents, track onboarding status, and receive automated notifications. PMO leaders need a first-pass delivery timeline that turns this BRD into execution artifacts.

Business Goals:
- Reduce manual onboarding coordination by 40 percent.
- Provide a single source of truth for onboarding status.
- Enable compliance and operations teams to review documents inside one workflow.

Scope:
- Conduct discovery and confirm onboarding workflow requirements.
- Design customer, operations, and compliance screens.
- Build secure document upload and metadata capture.
- Implement onboarding status workflow and notifications.
- Integrate with CRM account records.
- Test role-based access, file upload, and notification scenarios.
- Prepare launch readiness plan and stakeholder sign-off.

Project start date: 2026-08-03
Major launch milestone: 2026-11-27
Sprint duration: 2 weeks
Team availability: 4 developers, 2 QA, 1 UX designer, 1 PM, 1 Scrum Master

Milestones and deadlines:
- Requirements sign-off: 2026-08-21
- UX prototype approval: 2026-09-04
- CRM integration complete: 2026-10-16
- UAT complete: 2026-11-13
- Production launch: 2026-11-27

Dependency information:
- CRM API access is required before CRM integration can start.
- Compliance policy approval is required before document retention rules are finalized.
- Notification templates require marketing approval before UAT.

Timeline risks:
- CRM sandbox availability may delay integration testing.
- Compliance review can add rework if policies are not confirmed early.
- Customer pilot users may have limited availability during UAT.
